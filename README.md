## 🎯 What Is This?

The **LLM-Training-Handbook** is a complete, easy-to-understand technical guide that teaches you how large language models (the technology behind chatbots, writing assistants, and AI tools) are actually built and trained. 

Unlike typical tutorials that stop at basic theory, this handbook uses **real, documented training runs** from famous AI projects like Llama 3.1, DeepSeek-V3, and OPT-175B. By studying these actual examples, you'll learn to spot problems in AI training, understand why some models fail, and recognize the difference between a good and bad AI training process.

**Who is this for?** 
- Students curious about AI
- Developers wanting to understand AI training
- Researchers looking for practical references
- Anyone interested in how modern AI really works
